**Acuarelas Temáticas - EnamorARTE**Get ready to paint your heart out (literally!) at this fun watercolor workshop just in time for Valentine's Day! Laura Vicente will guide you through creating beautiful watercolor pieces perfect for gifting to someone special or keeping for yourself, because self-love counts too.Whether you've never picked up a paintbrush or you're a seasoned doodler, this session in Hoboken is all about having a good time while getting creative. Bring your Valentine's spirit and leave with artwork that's way better than anything you could find at the drugstore. Plus, there's something extra satisfying about saying "I made this!" when you hand someone your masterpiece.

*   [Get your spot here](https://luma.com/gxph9v7f)

![Image 7](https://watercolor.lauravicgal.art/assets/images/image09.jpg?v=8adba6dd)

**Creative Team Building: Art, Coffee & Connection**This workshop offers a relaxed, creative space where teams connect through art, culture, and conversation. Using the decoration of Italian moka pots, participants slow down, share ideas, and enjoy meaningful time together.

 Coffee brings people together, awakens the senses, and keeps you company.Every cup holds a story and a moment of calm.

**Watercolor Workshop for English-Speaking Union Immigrant Students**

 During this workshop, students painted Trees of Life as a visual representation of their immigration journeys. Through watercolor, reflection, and conversation, participants honored their heritage, resilience, and dreams while building meaningful connections with one another. I am deeply grateful to the English-Speaking Union for their support and for making this experience possible.
