Ceremonies

EnamorARTE

A unique engagement ceremony where the couple paints a shared canvas together, guided by an officiant. This intimate moment symbolizes unity, creativity, and the beginning of their journey. Expressing their love through art instead of words.

Sand

It's a symbolic ritual in which the couple pours different colored sands together, representing the joining of their lives, love, and families into a unified whole.

Handfasting

The word derives from the Old Norse term “handfesta", where you promise to love each other today, tomorrow, and forever.

The Planting

It is a symbolic ritual where the couple plants a tree or plant to represent the growth, strength, and care of their relationship

Time Capsule

It's a symbolic ritual in which the couple places letters, notes, or meaningful objects into a capsule to be opened in the future. It represents their commitment, memories, and hopes for a shared life, creating a lasting treasure to revisit and celebrate their love over time.

The Red Thread

It's a symbolic ritual rooted in East Asian folklore, particularly Chinese and Japanese traditions. During the ceremony, the couple ties a red string around their pinky fingers, symbolizing their unbreakable bond and the belief that fate has brought them together.
