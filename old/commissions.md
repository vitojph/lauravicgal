My work
-------

**Capture Drawing:** A watercolor straight from your travel journal, where every light stroke brings past moments to life. Let soft washes of color capture the soul of your adventures, turning memories into meaningful art with heart and personality.

_**Realistic Paintings:**_ Looking to capture the magic of a place that stole your heart on vacation? Or preserve the essence of something that’s been with you through life’s most meaningful moments? Let’s turn your memories into timeless watercolor treasures.

**Graphic Novel:** If there's a special experience you want to keep alive forever, tell me about it — and I’ll bring it to life with watercolor. Whether it's a single moment or a whole story, I can illustrate it like a page from your graphic novel — full of emotion, color, and meaning. Let your memory become a timeless visual tale.

**Keepsake Illustration:** Those shoes that walked every step of your journey. The teddy bear your baby holds close each night.

 Although the exact duration may vary, I always prioritize quality and attention to detail.

What materials will be used?
----------------------------

**Paper:** I work with professional, museum-grade 140 lb watercolor paper designed to last for decades.

**Watercolor:** I use only professional-grade watercolors from top industry brands to ensure vibrant and lasting colors.

**Tools:** I paint with high-quality natural and synthetic brushes, along with archival inks for lasting detail.
