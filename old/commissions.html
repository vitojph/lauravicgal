Watercolor Workshop for English-Speaking Union Immigrant Students
During this workshop, students painted Trees of Life as a visual representation of their immigration journeys. Through watercolor, reflection, and conversation, participants honored their heritage, resilience, and dreams while building meaningful connections with one another. I am deeply grateful to the English-Speaking Union for their support and for making this experience possible.
