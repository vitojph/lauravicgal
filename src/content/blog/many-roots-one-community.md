      Rooted in Art: Connecting Cultures and Building Community at HoLa Hoboken

      Hispanic culture inspires us to explore the rich stories, traditions, and roots of the many communities that shape our shared heritage. Recently, I had the privilege of putting that idea into practice alongside the vibrant student community at HoLa Hoboken.

      Using recycled materials, HoLa students brought a collective tree sculpture and mural to life—building it branch by branch through the imagination and enthusiasm of children across all age groups. Every single hand, image, and texture woven into this artwork represents a unique personal contribution to a larger, shared creation.

      Watching the kids collaborate, express their individual backgrounds, and discover how their stories intertwine was a powerful reminder of what community truly means. Art isn't just an aesthetic experience; it is a universal bridge that connects cultures, fosters empathy, and creates a sense of belonging.

      I am deeply grateful to the team at HoLa Hoboken for giving me the wonderful opportunity to lead this project and share with students the transformational power of art.

      Projects like this remind us that when we honor our diverse roots, we create something far stronger and more beautiful together. Let’s keep growing!
