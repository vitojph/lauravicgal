This spring, I’m excited to launch a new creative journey: *Watercolor Recipe Illustration: Art, Culture & Family Traditions.*

In this program, young artists explore food, memory, and identity through watercolor. They will paint ingredients, textures, colors, and flavors while creating their own illustrated recipe book: a meaningful keepsake that connects art, family, and cultural roots. Art becomes memory. Food becomes story.

I'm also exploring how this artistic approach could inspire new and creative ways to design menus or present product collections.
