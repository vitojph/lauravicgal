      I had the deep pleasure of accompanying The Committee for Hispanic
      Children and Families (CHCF) for a truly special maternity event dedicated
      to honoring mothers and mothers-to-be.


      The day was filled with warm surprises, creative moments, and an outpouring of love for every woman embarking on the wonderful, complex journey of bringing new life into the world. It was a rare and meaningful pause, a dedicated space for these mothers to relax, create, and simply take a moment for themselves amid the profound changes of pregnancy and early motherhood.


      Above all, it was a room full of shared connection. Motherhood is a transformative path, but it is never meant to be walked alone. Seeing these women come together to listen, support, and lift one another up was a beautiful reminder of a simple truth: when we have community, everything becomes easier.


      I am so grateful to CHCF for inviting me to be a part of this heart-centered gathering. Supporting women as they build their families, honor their strength, and create lasting bonds with one another is at the very core of why community spaces matter so much.
